# AubOS

AubOS delivers sovereign superintelligence for humans and AI-native organizations. AubOS extends current frontier ai capabilities with biomimetic memories, a self-improving software factory, ultra-tier subscription multiplexing, and a gorgeous visual control plane.

For one person, AubOS can become a lifelong partner in thinking, creating, deciding, and remembering. For an AI-native organization, it offers a unified coordinating fabric for people, agents, knowledge, and software.

## Vision

AubOS connects frontier models, conversations, tools, and workers into a system that builds continuity over time. It preserves sources, connects ideas, consolidates experiences, and carries learning forward.

AubOS Factory translates your vision into self-optimizing software, automatically parallelizing workers and multiplexing them across multiple frontier provider accounts for maximum return on token spend.

## Inspiration

AubOS draws inspiration from Paperclip, gBrain, and Gas City. We aspire to synthesize the strengths of each of these worthy projects into a turnkey jetpack for the human mind.

## Status

AubOS is bleeding-edge pre-alpha software. It does not work yet.

The architecture is under construction, features are incomplete, interfaces will break, and parts of the vision remain aspirational. Expect broken paths and rapid change. Do not entrust AubOS with important work or personal data yet.

Follow the roadmap to see what exists and what remains to be built.
