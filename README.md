# Vorton

Vorton delivers sovereign superintelligence for humans and AI-native organizations. Vorton extends current frontier AI capabilities with biomimetic memories, a self-improving software factory, ultra-tier subscription multiplexing, and an elegant visual control plane.

For one person, Vorton can become a lifelong partner in thinking, creating, deciding, and remembering. For an AI-native organization, it offers a unified coordinating fabric for people, agents, knowledge, and software.

## Product and installations

Vorton is the reusable upstream codebase, release line, and product platform. An installation supplies its own identity, data, policy, modules, workers, and infrastructure.

FreedOS is the Vorton installation that runs Freed. AubOS is the future Vorton installation that will run and manage Aubrey's personal life. Those names belong to the installations, not to forks of the platform.

Historical releases through 0.3.2 retain their original artifact and database identifiers so existing installations can upgrade without falsifying immutable manifests or rewriting applied migrations. New product surfaces use Vorton.

## Vision

Vorton connects frontier models, conversations, tools, and workers into a system that builds continuity over time. It preserves sources, connects ideas, consolidates experiences, and carries learnings forward.

Factory translates your visions into self-optimizing software, automatically parallelizing workers and multiplexing them across multiple frontier provider accounts for maximum return on token spend.

## Inspiration

Vorton draws inspiration from Paperclip, gBrain, and Gas City. We aspire to synthesize the strengths of each of these worthy projects into a turnkey jetpack for the human mind.

## Status

Vorton is bleeding-edge pre-alpha software. The architecture is under construction, features are incomplete, interfaces will break, and parts of the vision remain aspirational. Expect broken paths and rapid change. Do not entrust Vorton with important work or personal data yet.

Follow the roadmap to see what exists and what remains to be built.
